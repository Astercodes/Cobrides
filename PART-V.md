# Part V — Vendors, venues and service providers

## Discovery

The Vendors & venues screen has discovery, relationship/booking and coordination views. Search by name or services, specialist category, location, style, recorded unavailable date and maximum starting price. Prices can be sorted in the project base currency. A maximum-price filter includes only known prices in that currency; it never silently compares currencies at an assumed rate. Venue capacity, services, starting price and an HTTPS portfolio URL can be recorded. Listing images are illustrative category images.

Four clearly labelled fictional sample listings provide an interactive discovery experience. Samples can be shortlisted once per project; they cannot be used to create booking records. Real professionals use separate personal records. No search result claims verified reviews or confirmed availability. A date without a recorded conflict remains unconfirmed. A production marketplace, verified identities, live calendars and authenticated reviews require external services and a backend.

## Relationships and bookings

Professional profiles are canonical stakeholder records, including assigned celebrations and unavailable dates. Record inquiry, availability inquiry, consultation, negotiation and progress notes, with supporting files. Local communication spaces reuse the existing conversation hub.

Proposals include subtotal, currency and manual exchange-rate snapshot, tax, service/fixed charges, scope, service date, expiry and cancellation terms. Select up to three proposals for comparison. A recorded external booking creates exactly one linked expense, with charges and snapshot preserved. Duplicate proposal bookings, conflicting active vendor/celebration bookings, expired proposals, declined proposals, fictional samples and recorded unavailable service dates are blocked. Booked proposals remain preserved; new commercial terms require a new proposal.

A booking opens its canonical budget cost and deposit/installment editor. Contract documents and externally signed status are local records, not electronic signatures. Cancellation cancels the linked expense and schedules while retaining payment/refund history; a penalty is a separate Cancellation expense. Completed service records permit private personal performance reviews. These reviews are not published or independently verified.

## Collaboration and dependencies

The vendor board uses the existing Planning tasks, rather than duplicated deliverables. It supports assigned vendors (including venues and planners), owners, dates, priorities, dependencies, approval records, attachments, local discussions and progress columns. Existing completion and prerequisite rules apply across views.

Shared logistics instructions contain one canonical title, celebration, venue-local date/time, location, instructions, assigned participants and prerequisite instructions. Every participant sees the same local record. Acknowledgements are recorded by the current user on behalf of named participants. Completion requires all current-version acknowledgements, completed prerequisites and no recorded participant date conflicts.

Revising an instruction increments its version, preserves prior instructions in history and resets its completion and acknowledgements. Dependent instructions also receive a new version and lose stale acknowledgements. Completed dependents must be reopened first. Cyclic dependencies are blocked. Coordination briefs can be downloaded for manual sharing; nothing is delivered automatically.

Change requests and issue escalations include a responsible owner, priority, deadline, requested approver, resolution state, notes and files. Material edits invalidate an earlier recorded approval. A change can create one linked follow-up task in both the vendor board and Planning. Changes do not silently rewrite commercial terms, actual costs or other instructions.

## Boundaries and validation

All records remain browser-local. There is no vendor login, shared database, notification delivery, actual booking, contract acceptance or authenticated approval. Supporting documents use the existing three-files / 200 KB-per-file limit and full JSON export. No new external integrations or paid services were added.

Model checks cover migration, availability semantics, safe portfolio URLs, linked currency/charge calculations, duplicate/expired/sample booking guards, participant validation, acknowledgement deduplication, version history, cyclic dependencies and downstream invalidation. Browser checks cover discovery filters, vendor creation, proposal comparison and booking, finance handoffs, canonical deliverables, logistics completion/revision/persistence, escalation-task deduplication, and desktop/mobile layout.
