Common questions

How do I create an account or sign in?

Choose Start planning to create your account, or Sign in if you already have one. Follow any email verification instructions shown. If you cannot remember your password, use Forgot password on the sign-in page.

How do I find my plans on another device?

Sign in with the same account you used to create them. Before moving devices, check that your latest changes have finished saving. If a saving error appears, keep the current page open and use the retry option before signing out or clearing site data.

How do I join a wedding team?

Open the invitation sent by the bride or planner and sign in with the email address that was invited. Your access depends on your assigned permissions. If the invitation is no longer valid, ask the person who invited you for a new one.

Why does a product or social link have no preview?

Some websites restrict previews or require their own sign-in. Check that you pasted an individual public item or post link. You can retain the original source link even when its preview is unavailable. Playback and availability can also depend on the source platform.

What should I do if an upload fails?

Check the file type, size and storage allowance shown in the upload flow, and make sure your connection is working. Keep your original file. If it still fails, contact us with the file type and the error message; you do not need to send the private file itself.

How do I request access to or deletion of my information?

Contact us from your account email and explain your request. We may need to verify your identity. Our Privacy notice explains how information is handled and how to make a deletion request.
