# Private beauty appointment scheduling

Run supabase/private-appointments.sql after private-care.sql. Existing private appointment definitions remain in preparationRecords. Appointment cards gain Appointment details for duration (15–480 minutes), IANA timezone, external booking reference and booking/attendance status. Source date/time/provider/location changes require another review of the scheduling record. These are account-private records, independent of provider booking systems and personal preparation/task completion. No provider is contacted, no booking is made/cancelled externally, and no charge or payment is inferred.

Owner-only RPCs read current appointment definitions from the authenticated user's saved workspace, including separate personal/demo envelopes. Per-account locking prevents concurrent confirmation races, and locking the workspace head prevents definition changes during validation. Confirmed appointments cannot overlap another current confirmed appointment in the same scope, including other private wedding projects. Draft, cancelled and source-stale records do not reserve time. Nonexistent local times at daylight-saving transitions and future attendance reports are rejected. PostgreSQL resolves ambiguous fall-back wall times to its standard offset; exports use the resulting saved UTC instant. Exact retry IDs and expected revisions protect changes from multiple devices. Raw scheduling, write and history tables have no direct client grants or anonymous access.

Calendar export rechecks the current record before writing a private ICS file. UTC start/end, duration, title and location are included; booking references and private notes are omitted. Text is escaped and lines folded to the calendar format. This is a user-selected export, not live calendar synchronization or delivered reminders.

Tests: private-appointments.sql.test.cjs (ownership, retries, revisions, overlaps, schedule review, timezone/DST and attendance guards); private-appointments.ui.test.cjs (mobile controls, exact retry and UTC calendar export). Existing private-care tests also pass. Live real-account multi-device testing remains deferred by the user.
