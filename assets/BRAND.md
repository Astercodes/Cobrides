# CoBrides visual identity

The forever ribbon is an original SVG F/B monogram: an upright folded ribbon forms the F, a teal returning loop suggests the B and the continuity of a bridal journey, and a small golden spark marks its beginning. It uses the supplied magenta, orange, teal, purple, and gold palette. The vector is used by the landing page, workspace, and favicon.

The five new editorial photographs were supplied by the user. WebP assets are optimized derivatives of those PNG files; originals are preserved in Downloads. Wedding embrace: hero and illustrative wedding website. Proposal: Vision Studio. Seaside retreat: celebrations. Planning together: people. Honeymoon: evolving journey.

Landing content is adapted from the user-supplied CoBrides copy. Planned capabilities are labelled to distinguish them from the browser-local personal prototype. Social profiles and company contact destinations were not supplied, so no placeholder external links are published.
