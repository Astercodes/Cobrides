# Registry product references

Verified on 2026-10-16 from official retailer pages. Product names, destination links and remote preview image URLs are in `registry-catalog.js`. Images remain hosted by the retailer and are used as source-linked product previews; no retailer photographs were copied into this repository. Retailer trademarks and photographs belong to their owners.

Catalog: ten IKEA products and the KitchenAid Artisan 5 Quart stand mixer with Premium Accessory Pack, Empire Red. These are product references for the example wedding registry, not claims of stock, current prices, bookings or completed purchases. Availability, shipping regions and checkout are controlled by each retailer.

Imported products use the existing `/api/link-preview` endpoint, which pins public DNS results and validates redirects. A blocked preview leaves the source URL available for an editable entry and an uploaded photograph. Registry records only marked Public enter guest website links. Giver identities, private delivery records and thank-you drafts are excluded.

Guest sites are public snapshots carried in a compressed URL fragment. They need no account to view; changing a plan requires sharing a new link. Large uploaded images may exceed the share-link limit, in which case the interface explains the limit and offers a downloadable website. Live shared reservations, revocable hosted URLs and automatic retailer stock/order synchronization require persistent services and are not claimed by this interface.
