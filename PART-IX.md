# Part IX — Optional personal preparation

Modules 26 and 27 add separate, independently enabled Beauty & Self-care and Marriage & Home Preparation spaces. Neither creates records until the user enables it and chooses what to add. Wording and categories make no assumption about relationship structure, faith, culture, name changes, counseling or legal procedures.

Beauty preparation covers user-entered hair, skincare, makeup trials, nail/spa appointments, product lists, self-care and wellness goals, check-ins and emergency-kit checklists. Marriage/home preparation covers optional counseling or courses, financial/household planning, documentation checklist labels, legal appointments, optional name-change plans, relocation, home setup and post-wedding administration. Editable kit/home templates add only general planning ideas.

Appointments have local times, location, provider references and external booking status. Dates may be manual or relative to a wedding/celebration date. Routines have private date-stamped check-ins, configurable intervals and next dates; duplicate same-day check-ins are blocked. Goals have user-defined units and progress. Checklists retain completed items through edits and cannot complete with outstanding steps.

Records stay outside general tasks unless explicitly connected. Linked tasks use canonical project scheduling and existing dependency/approval guards; personal notes are never copied to those tasks. Routine check-ins remain in the personal workspace. Linking a cost to Budget is a separate action and creates one estimate; subsequent payments and budget edits remain canonical in Finance. No booking, payment or provider notification is made. Shopping links show the inventory status of selected real products without creating duplicate purchases or expenses.

Private calendar downloads include pending appointments/tasks and routine recurrence, local floating appointment times, a one-day display alarm and CLASS:PRIVATE. They exclude personal notes. Importing and notification behavior depend on the user's calendar app. The app itself has no background, email or push reminders.

Privacy boundary: entries are excluded from public wedding website/registry exports, but are still browser-local records, not authenticated or encrypted private workspaces. Full journey backup exports include these records. Avoid identity-document uploads or confidential counseling content; this version stores only text planning records and has no document-upload control. Enforced partner privacy, secure document storage, shared finance collaboration and production scheduling require a backend.

The thirty-module inventory places these at 26–27. Travel planning remains in guest guidance/logistics, and post-wedding preparation is included in module 27. The remaining roadmap modules are traditions, wedding-day operations and memories.

Validation: static build and all existing suites plus preparation tests for opt-in task links, note isolation, event date changes, routine interval/check-in guards, goal/checklist completion, budget idempotence and calendar privacy. Browser checks cover relative appointment creation, budget connection, routine check-in, emergency kit completion guard, household checklist, module count and mobile overflow.
