# Part X — Wedding-day execution and post-wedding management

Module 28 adds an operator-updated, event-specific control room with a run of show, responsible coordinators, linked planning tasks, activity dependencies, required professionals, readiness check-ins, setup inspections, incidents, contingency actions, local escalation/acknowledgment records, passenger-capacity planning and manual transport statuses. Confirmed guest arrival flags are shared with Seating & Hospitality. Run-of-show CSV downloads support coordinator handoffs.

An incident can reference a professional and activity, record delay minutes and intended recipients, and create an explicitly unsent local alert. Linking a delayed professional updates that event's readiness record. Suggested time adjustments propagate through dependent activities, are reviewed before application, reject stale revisions and duplicate application, and cannot cross the event day boundary. Original/revised times remain on the incident. Applying a suggestion does not reschedule vendor bookings or general planning tasks and does not notify anyone. Completing activities respects required vendor readiness, inspection checks, predecessor completion and linked task approvals/dependencies.

Module 29 adds private browser-local albums, photograph uploads (three files per record, 200 KB each), guest contributor attribution, external video links, photographer/filmmaker deliverables, operator-entered media approvals/change requests, resubmission review reset and self-contained approved keepsake downloads. Edits preserve revision metadata; replacement photos replace prior stored files. Deliverable status is derived from its linked media. A conservative overall media-storage guard limits browser storage growth. Public wedding website exports do not include these albums automatically.

Post-wedding closeout reads operational incidents, unapproved deliveries/media, received-gift thank-you status, remaining expense forecasts and shopping return/exchange records. Links lead to existing vendor reviews, acknowledgments, payment reconciliation and commerce workflows. Anniversary calculation uses the wedding project date, with a calendar download for the next anniversary (Feb 29 anniversaries use Feb 28 in non-leap years). Calendar notifications depend on importing into an external calendar application.

Archiving marks the project Archived locally and downloads a private project JSON snapshot. Outstanding closeout items require an explicit exception note. Archive records preserve the unresolved counts and note. Reopening preserves archive history. Archiving does not settle payments, complete open work, cancel bookings, delete content or lock other workspaces. A downloaded snapshot is a backup artifact, not a cloud restore service.

Production boundaries: no real-time synchronization, authentication/authorized live access, external incident delivery, GPS tracking, shared album service, guest upload endpoint, QR photo collection, large cloud media storage, automated anniversary notifications or immutable cloud archives are connected. All operating statuses, approvals and acknowledgments are entered by the current browser operator. Intended recipients do not create access permissions or delivery receipts. Guest-attributed uploads are entered by the operator; they do not prove who submitted a file.

Validation: all existing model suites and static build; new operations tests cover dependency cycles, readiness/completion guards, reviewed delay propagation, revision conflicts, duplicate application, project/event isolation, local alert state, transport capacity/duplicate passenger checks, anniversary dates, deliverable approval and archive exceptions. Browser tests cover setup guards, readiness, a 30-minute makeup delay shifting dependent photography, transport departures/arrivals, photo upload, delivery submission/approval/change requests, resubmission approval reset, archive guard and both mobile layouts. The approved keepsake download was inspected for its embedded photo and absence of private gift ledger data.

The archive exception download was inspected for project-scoped records, approved media and preserved unresolved issue counts; reopening retained its history.
