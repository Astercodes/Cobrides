A LITTLE CLARITY

Choose with
confidence.

Do the paid plans include different features?

No. All four paid durations include the premium feature collection. The difference is how much planning time you choose.

Which four features are free?

Vision Studio, Planning & Tasks, Timeline & Countdowns, and Guest List. Their included tools are marked Free in the feature guide.

Can I start before I am engaged?

Absolutely. Begin with inspiration, your priorities and a little preparation. Your journey can evolve as your plans become more definite.

Can I view prices in another currency?

Use the country and currency control in the header. Plan prices originate in naira; converted amounts are estimates.

Is payment taken when I choose a plan?

No. Plan selection currently opens your workspace. Paid activation and the four-feature access limit will take effect once billing is connected.

Can my wedding team work with me?

CoBrides includes a separate shared workspace for invitations, assigned responsibilities and team messages. Shared accounts must be activated, and external providers need their own authorized connections.
