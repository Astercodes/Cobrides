# Retailer shopping edit

Shopping & commerce → Shop by retailer now offers 18 curated product references: six ASOS and twelve Fashion Nova. Categories cover bridal outfits, bridesmaid styles, shoes, bags, accessories and honeymoon wear. Store selection, category filters, search, alphabetical sorting, saved-first sorting, product details and external shopping links work on mobile and desktop. Discovery replaces the fictional editorial cards; existing saved personal records remain intact.

`retailer-catalog.js` contains original retailer URLs, retailer-hosted image references, observed native prices/currencies and capture dates. ASOS facts were checked against its individual product pages. Fashion Nova facts came from the public JSON-LD on its honeymoon and handbags collections. Descriptions and marketing copy are not copied. Catalog product URLs are ordinary product references, without affiliate tracking. This is a curated selection, not the retailers' complete catalogs or a continuous stock feed. Prices are labeled snapshots; country, size, delivery and current price are confirmed at the retailer. Add or refresh curated products by reviewing their original product metadata and updating this catalog.

Retailer images stay on their original hosts. Fashion Nova photos rendered in Chrome during verification. ASOS's image host rejected the verification requests, including its exact original source URL format. ASOS image URLs remain accurate references, with a clearly labeled retailer-photo fallback rather than substituted or fabricated product photography. Approved catalog/feed access would be needed to guarantee ASOS photography across clients. No paid scraper or unapproved authenticated access was added.

Save to my list opens the existing commerce editor rather than silently creating a purchase. Users choose lists, quantities, sizes and timing. Public product image/price metadata is preserved on the resulting ordinary shopping record. Existing saved products open their current record instead of producing another identical item. Existing commerce validation still guards distinct variants, positive quantities and exchange rates. When a conversion rate is unavailable and the product currency differs from the wedding currency, the rate field is left blank and required; a false 1:1 rate is never inserted. Currency display uses existing country/currency preferences while native price currency stays explicit. No budget expense, payment, order or refund is created merely by saving.

Refresh product details reuses the authenticated link-preview endpoint and its existing request limits. Failed refreshes retain the last price snapshot. A successful refresh can update the image or native price; the user still reviews it before saving. Account/project changes during a request prevent the result being applied to another workspace. No retailer credentials, catalog-feed accounts or new SQL setup are needed for this curated version. Saved products use existing account-scoped cloud storage.

Validation: retailer-shopping.test.cjs plus commerce.test.cjs and link-preview.test.cjs pass (five reported tests, no failures/skips). Coverage includes safe/distinct source references, category/store filters, product details, failed refreshes, reviewed saving, image/price persistence through the real commerce save handler, exchange-rate handling, duplicate reuse, authenticated asset loading and a 390-pixel viewport without horizontal overflow. Actual Fashion Nova photos and the mobile catalog were also checked in Chrome. Build and syntax checks pass. No live customer account data was changed during validation.
