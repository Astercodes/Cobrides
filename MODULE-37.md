# Module 37 — Wedding Experience & Entertainment Management

Dedicated primary navigation with eighteen permanently visible subpages: Overview; Food & Catering; Drinks & Bar; Cake & Desserts; Music & DJ; MC & Hosting; Live Entertainment; Screens & Displays; Lighting & AV; Guest Activities; Special Moments; Run of Show; Hospitality; Vendors; Budget; Inspiration; Live Operations; Experience Archive.

Each celebration owns its experience vision, budget target, service capacity, contingency reserve and scoped records. Sample projects receive twenty-eight editable plans per celebration without replacing personal projects or creating financial transactions.

Records support named owners/professionals/approvers, preparation dates and service times, specifications/menus/cue notes, capacity assumptions, source links, readiness checklists, statuses and explicit connections to canonical tasks, operations cues and budget expenses. Approved material changes reopen approval. Completion requires readiness checks. Linked costs preserve financial terms; repeated linking reuses one expense. Existing catering, cake and music allowances are reused when scoped to the event.

Food and hospitality use event-specific confirmed guests, meal selections and counts of private needs from Guest Management. Private details remain in the guest workspace. Run of Show and Live Operations use original operation activities, readiness, inspection, incident and delay workflows. Their existing completion/revision safeguards remain active. Experience records participate in global search, relationships, activity and the unified calendar; linked tasks/cues prevent duplicate calendar entries.

Vision Studio, supplier proposals, contracts, documents, conversations, payment milestones and media archives remain in their original connected workspaces. The initial module provides editable service/production briefs and local planning workflows; it does not implement live provider discovery, authorized streaming, guest-upload portals, broadcasts, AI generation, equipment protocols or actual shared-account communication. User-configured references can be stored without inventing provider access. Music rights and equipment specifications must be confirmed with the relevant qualified providers. No payments, bookings or commands are issued.

Validation covers section/event/project scope, named approvals, readiness completion, safe source URLs, cost-link idempotence, cancellation exclusions, and sample seeding. Existing suite and classic-script compatibility checks remain required.
