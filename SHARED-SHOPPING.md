# Shared shopping and purchase tracking

Apply `supabase/shared-shopping.sql` after shared-ledger.sql and collaboration-access.sql. The migration is repeatable and does not publish or rewrite personal shopping/wardrobe data. The standalone setup copy is `C:/Users/ayeni/Downloads/cobridey-shared-shopping.sql`.

Brides open Shopping & commerce → Team shopping. Existing personal item cards offer Share with shopping team; the review form copies only product identity, retailer image/link, variant, quantity and priority. Personal notes, private uploads, measurements, prices, attachments and payment records are not copied. Users choose a destination list and review its audience before saving. Collaborators open Team shopping from their shared workspace. The standalone route is protected by the normal account gate.

Planning writers create named lists and choose accepted additional readers. Other authorized wedding managers also have access. Assigned buyers must already be accepted and able to read the list. Readers see the shared list, but only assigned buyers or planning writers can update purchase progress. Buyers follow the Needed → Reserved/Purchased → Shipped/Delivered → Return requested → Returned → Refunded lifecycle. Planning writers can correct statuses with a required progress note. Buyer reassignment, audience removal, expired membership, project archival and list archival are enforced server-side. Refunded/cancelled items cannot be changed by a nonmanager. Changes are serialised through project locks and guarded by optimistic revisions and exact-request UUID journals. Immutable revision snapshots retain history; there are no direct authenticated/anonymous table grants.

Duplicate active product links are rejected within a list. Amazon dp/gp-product links normalize to ASIN identity so tracking-query variations do not create another item. Other HTTPS links compare exact source URLs after fragments are removed. This does not identify equivalent products across unrelated retailer URLs or lists. Cancelling an item releases its product identity; reactivating it rechecks duplicates. Titles, quantities, dates, URLs and field limits are validated. There are at most 100 shared lists per wedding and 200 items per list; the workspace fetches items only for the selected list rather than all lists at once.

Product previews reuse the existing authenticated link-reader endpoint. Returned titles/images are reviewed before sharing. No merchant accounts are signed into and no order is placed. Product image links are external retailer references. Private storage objects require a separate permission-controlled sharing workflow and are not automatically copied from personal records.

Only users with explicit finance plus planning access can link a shopping item to an existing expense in the same wedding. Expense identifiers and choices are omitted from nonfinancial responses. Linking never creates a second expense, payment, refund or financial contribution. Purchase/refund statuses are operational records and do not change the append-only payment journal. The expense ledger remains the source of financial totals. The subsequent retail-expenses.sql migration adds reviewed standalone retailer expenses and atomic team-item cost links; see RETAIL-EXPENSES.md. Carrier synchronization, merchant return submission and retailer transactions remain separate integrations.

The runner requires both dependencies to avoid silently skipped checks. Build and syntax checks pass. Live multi-account/device testing remains deferred by the user.
