# Marriage home & household setup
Module 32 is a separate optional Home Setup segment. Local housing preferences, property comparisons and viewings; move-in countdown; setup budget and room progress; household needs; moving/delivery plans; utility/service plans; responsibilities; safety/maintenance; design inspiration links and layout notes. Editable essentials starters deduplicate by title.

Existing shopping items connect by purchaseId. Owned/delivered/received registry inventory drives room readiness, including returns. One purchase can satisfy one active home need; commerce/registry preserve their duplicate purchase guards. Finance links are explicit and idempotent; linked purchases use the commerce expense rather than creating a second ledger expense. Property comparisons do not inflate setup spending. Contributions, registry, documents, transition preparation and shopping remain canonical in their original modules.

Search/activity index household records; dated setup records and move-in dates appear in the unified calendar. No public website export includes home records. This is local planning, without authenticated partner sharing, property discovery, universal retailer imports, service booking, external delivery updates, mortgage calculations or document security. Housing costs are recorded plans, not affordability recommendations. Store documentation references rather than confidential identity or mortgage documents.


Home samples seed once per sample wedding: thirty-seven records across property comparisons, viewings, household needs, moving/delivery, utilities/services, responsibilities, safety/maintenance and design. All eight room categories have needs. Six existing registry purchases are reused so received and partial gifts affect readiness without duplicate shopping records. Property comparisons and cancelled delivery estimates stay outside setup totals. No property application, financial transaction or service booking occurs; personal plans remain intact.
