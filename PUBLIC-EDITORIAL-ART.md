# Public-page editorial artwork

Created with the built-in image-generation tool. These are illustrative editorial compositions, not photographs of listed vendors, products for sale, or actual customer celebrations. No customer information was used.

## Saved assets and final prompt set

- `assets/editorial/professionals.webp`: Premium editorial still-life photograph for Cobridey's professionals section. A black mirrorless camera, florist shears, coral linen, burnt-orange ranunculus, deep rose petals, cream event-brief paper without readable text and brass clips. Warm ivory studio surface, soft morning light, natural textures, generous negative space. No people, hands, logos, screens or synthetic 3D finish.
- `assets/editorial/celebration.webp`: Luxury reception place setting on ivory linen with a bentwood chair, coral-orange flowers and raspberry roses, cream ceramics, peach napkin, ribbed glass, brass cutlery and tapered candles. Intimate composition, soft daylight and realistic textures. No people, text, grandiose venue or fantasy sunset.
- `assets/editorial/bridal-details.webp`: Ivory satin bridal slingbacks, a coral structured evening clutch, sculptural pearl earrings, silk ribbon and tulle on a pale peach surface. Clear accessories, balanced asymmetric styling, natural shadows and authentic textile detail. No brands, words, people or plastic rendering.
- `assets/editorial/travel.webp`: Sunlit travel still life with coral luggage, straw hat, tortoiseshell sunglasses, cream linen shirt and unmarked rose notebook. Warm ivory wall, bougainvillea shadows, natural materials and generous negative space. No people, passports, tickets, text or brands.
- `assets/editorial/stationery.webp`: Blank cream cotton-paper invitation with an embossed botanical border, coral envelope, unmarked cream wax seal, rose silk ribbon and orange ranunculus. Tactile flat lay with realistic fibers and soft directional light. No writing, names, logos or people.

All prompts requested wide 3:2 editorial compositions in warm ivory, coral, orange and rose. Originals were copied from the image-generation output into the project, optimized as WebP and inspected before use.

## Visual hierarchy

The landing page uses exactly three unique product snapshots: Central in the hero, Vision Studio beside inspiration, and Budget in the finance section. No product snapshot is repeated on the landing page. Professionals, bridal details, celebrations, stationery and travel use distinct editorial artwork. Coordination and anticipation use native accessible diagrams rather than additional screenshots. Features keeps its two unique hero screenshots and uses editorial imagery in selected categories. People uses the professionals composition.
