# Newlywed transition & first-year life
Module 36, separate segment. Dashboard reads existing task counts, finance closeout, received-gift acknowledgments, media deliverables, returns, legal follow-ups and home inventory. Sources remain canonical; changing first-year focus does not archive or delete wedding records.

Eight first-year categories: transition tasks, monthly household budget, savings goals, routines, milestones/celebrations, travel/experiences, traditions/holidays and enrichment resources. Monthly income/expense plans are separate from wedding ledger obligations; annual spending is monthly plan times twelve, not actual spending. Savings are self-reported. Recurring completions advance dates without duplicate check-ins. Dated plans feed unified calendar/search/activity; budgets and savings do not create calendar entries.

Anniversary countdown uses existing anniversary date handling, including Feb 29 fallback. First-year marriage date can override project date. Transition starters are editable and deduplicated. Attire preservation, printing, resale and donations are tracking tasks only. Existing memories/approved keepsakes/archive, home, honeymoon, registry, commerce, relationship and optional faith modules remain connected by navigation.

Browser-local only: no bank connection, authenticated partner sharing, automatic notifications, continuous external calendar sync, album printing service, resale publishing or spiritual-group communication. Financial plans do not move money or imply settlement. Local full journey backups include these records.
