# Marriage documentation & legal preparation
Module 35 is a dedicated administrative segment. Seven categories cover civil/religious registration, supporting-document references, appointments/officiants/witnesses, name/account updates, cross-border translations/authentication, independent legal consultations and post-wedding administration. Editable starters are research prompts, not jurisdiction-specific legal requirements.

Records carry manual status, official-source HTTPS reference and date checked, appointment/application reference, deadlines, expiry metadata, checklist and fee estimate. Changing an existing source resets its prior checked date. Checklist completion is guarded; unchecking reopens a completed record. Fee links create one canonical finance expense and never imply payment. Local counters report overdue and 30-day expiration windows.

No legal requirements database, legal advice, appointment booking, application submission, automatic reminders, professional discovery feed or secure document vault is connected. No identity-document upload or identity-number field is provided. Legal records are excluded from general search/activity, shared calendar, day packs and public website exports. Full journey backup includes them; local storage is neither encrypted nor authenticated. Qualified professionals and official authorities remain the verification source.
